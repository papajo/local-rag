# Confidentiality and document storage (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. Nothing in this file describes a real firm, a real client, or a real case.

## Where client documents are stored

Client documents are stored locally, on the firm's own office systems.
Nothing is uploaded to a public cloud service and nothing is sent to
an outside AI service. Documents are stored in the firm's case files,
and access is limited to the attorney and paralegal assigned to the case.

## Who can see a client's file

Only the attorney and paralegal assigned to the case can open a
client's file. No other staff member, and no outside party, is given
access unless the client gives written permission or a court orders it.

## What happens when a case closes

When a case closes, the file is kept for seven years, then destroyed
securely. A client may request a copy of their own file at any time
during those seven years.
