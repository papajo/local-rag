# Intake checklist — uncontested divorce (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. Nothing in this file describes a real firm, a real client, or a real case.

## Documents needed for an uncontested divorce intake

An uncontested divorce turns on both spouses agreeing on property, custody,
and support. The intake cannot be completed until the firm has:

1. A copy of the marriage certificate.
2. A completed financial affidavit from each spouse, listing income, debts,
   and monthly expenses.
3. A property list covering the family home, vehicles, bank accounts,
   retirement accounts, and any debts in either spouse's name.
4. A draft parenting plan, if the couple has minor children, covering
   custody, visitation, and child support.
5. A signed intake form with current contact details for both spouses.

## What happens if a document is missing

The firm keeps one missing-document list per case. Each row shows what is
missing, who owes it, and how long it has been waited for. The case does
not move to drafting until the property list and both financial affidavits
are received. The firm chases missing documents by phone and email, and
the list is reviewed every Monday.

## Who handles intake

Intake is handled by the firm's paralegal, who checks the documents
against this checklist and flags anything incomplete before the file
goes to an attorney.
