# Fee policy (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. The fees below are invented sample figures for the demo only.
> They are not the fees of any real firm.

## Sample fee schedule

- Free initial consultation: no charge, up to 30 minutes.
- Uncontested divorce, flat fee: $1,500, payable in two instalments —
  $750 at engagement and $750 before filing.
- Document review, hourly: $200 per hour, billed in 15-minute units.
- All fees are confirmed in writing in the engagement letter before
  any work begins. No work is billed before the engagement letter
  is signed.

## What the flat fee covers

The uncontested divorce flat fee covers the intake review, drafting
of the petition and settlement agreement, and filing with the court.
It does not cover a contested hearing, mediation, or an appeal. If
a case becomes contested, the firm quotes a new fee in writing before
continuing.
