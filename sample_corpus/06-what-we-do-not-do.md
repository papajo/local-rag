# What the firm does not take on (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. Nothing in this file describes a real firm, a real client, or a real case.

## Matters outside the firm's practice

The firm handles family law and estate planning only. It does not take
on criminal defence, personal injury, immigration, or bankruptcy cases.
A caller with one of those matters is given the name of the local bar
association referral service instead of a consultation.

## What the firm will not do

The firm will not give legal advice by phone or email to someone who
is not yet a client. It will not quote a fee for a matter it does not
handle, and it will not confirm a deadline for a case it has not
reviewed. Any caller asking for one of those things is asked to book
a consultation or to contact the appropriate service.
