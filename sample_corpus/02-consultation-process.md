# Consultation process (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. Nothing in this file describes a real firm, a real client, or a real case.

## How a free consultation starts

A free consultation starts by phone call, by email, or through the
contact form on the firm's website. The caller is asked for their name,
a phone number, the type of matter, and a short description of the issue.

## What to bring to the first consultation

Clients should bring photo identification, any court papers they have
already received, and the documents listed on the intake checklist for
their matter. For a divorce consultation, that means the marriage
certificate and the financial affidavit, if they have them already.
Bringing the documents to the first meeting saves a second round of
chasing them later.

## What happens in the consultation

An attorney personally evaluates the case in the consultation and
explains the options, the likely steps, and the fee. No legal advice
is given before the consultation, and no advice is given by phone or
email to someone who is not yet a client. After the consultation, the
client receives a written summary of the options discussed and the
next steps if they choose to proceed.
