# Deadline and response policy (SAMPLE — fictional firm)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Harbor & Pine Law is a fictional firm created for this
> demo. Nothing in this file describes a real firm, a real client, or a real case.

## Response times

The firm responds to a new inquiry within one business day. Existing
clients receive a response to a message within two business days.
Urgent messages — for example, a court date within 7 days — are flagged
for same-day review by the attorney handling the case.

## Deadlines

Every case has a deadline calendar. The calendar tracks filing deadlines,
court dates, and document due dates, with reminders at 60 days, 30 days,
and 7 days before each deadline. The deadline calendar is reviewed every
Monday, and any deadline within 7 days is reviewed daily until it is met.

## What this policy does not do

This policy states how the firm tracks its own deadlines. It does not
state the deadline for any specific client's case. A client's own
deadline is confirmed only in writing by their attorney, after the
attorney has reviewed that client's file. Never rely on a general
policy document for a case deadline.
