# Food distribution (SAMPLE — fictional nonprofit)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Bayside Community Pantry is a fictional nonprofit
> invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real organisation or person.

## Who can receive food

Any household in the pantry's service area can receive food. Households
register once with a name and a ZIP code; no income documents are required.

## How often a household can visit

Each registered household may visit the pantry twice a month. Emergency
boxes are available once between regular visits, with no questions asked.

## What this page does not cover

This page describes the general distribution rules. It does not state the
date of any specific upcoming distribution event. Event dates are posted
on the pantry door and announced by the volunteer coordinator.
