# Volunteer onboarding (SAMPLE — fictional nonprofit)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Bayside Community Pantry is a fictional nonprofit
> invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real organisation or person.

## Steps to become a volunteer

New volunteers complete four steps before their first shift:

1. Fill in the online volunteer application.
2. Attend a 45-minute orientation, held on the first Saturday of each month.
3. Sign the volunteer agreement and the confidentiality pledge.
4. Pick a first shift with the volunteer coordinator.

## Minimum age for volunteers

Volunteers must be at least 16 years old. Volunteers aged 14 or 15 may
help only alongside a parent or guardian who is also a registered volunteer.

## Who coordinates volunteers

The volunteer coordinator schedules shifts, runs orientation, and keeps
the volunteer roster. Shift changes go to the coordinator at least 24 hours
before the shift.
