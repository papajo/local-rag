# Client privacy and records (SAMPLE — fictional nonprofit)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Bayside Community Pantry is a fictional nonprofit
> invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real organisation or person.

## Where household records are kept

Household registration records are kept in a locked filing cabinet and in
one password-protected spreadsheet on the office computer. They are not
kept in any cloud service.

## How long records are kept

Household records are kept for three years after the household's last
visit, then shredded or deleted.

## Sharing records with partners

The pantry shares household counts with partner food banks, never names
or addresses. A household's record is shared only with that household's
written consent.
