# Donations policy (SAMPLE — fictional nonprofit)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Bayside Community Pantry is a fictional nonprofit
> invented for this demo. The figures below are invented sample figures.

## Food donations we accept

The pantry accepts unopened, unexpired shelf-stable food. Fresh produce is
accepted on Tuesday and Thursday mornings only, so it can be handed out
the same day. Home-cooked food and opened packages cannot be accepted.

## Donation receipts

Every cash donation of $25 or more receives a written receipt within
5 business days. Receipts for food donations list the items and weight,
not a dollar value; the donor sets the value.

## Restricted gifts

A donor may restrict a gift to one program. Restricted gifts are tracked in
a separate ledger line and reported to the board every quarter.
