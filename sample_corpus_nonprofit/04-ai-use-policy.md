# AI use policy (SAMPLE — fictional nonprofit)

> SAMPLE DATA ONLY. Bayside Community Pantry is a fictional nonprofit
> invented for this demo. Nothing here describes a real organisation or person.

## Approved AI uses

Staff may use AI tools to draft newsletters, summarise meeting notes, and
answer general questions from the pantry's own public documents. A person
reviews every AI draft before it is sent outside the pantry.

## What staff must never put into an AI tool

Staff must never paste client names, addresses, phone numbers, or household
details into an AI tool. Donor card numbers and bank details are never
entered either.

## Who approves new AI tools

The executive director approves any new AI tool before staff use it, after
checking where the tool stores data.
